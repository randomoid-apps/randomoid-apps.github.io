Common questions

My music stops instead of playing under the coach.

Start your music first, then play the workout in Overtone. If it still pauses your music, open Overtone's Settings and, under Other Audio, set Mode to Automatic or Play Over It.

The library says it couldn't load the workouts.

Overtone needs a connection to list and add workouts from the library. Those you've added already play without one.

The Apple Watch app doesn't show what's playing.

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Overtone under Available Apps, and install it.
