Overtone Workouts Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Overtone doesn't collect any information about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you do in it is sent to us.

What stays on your iPhone

Your programs, workouts, audio files, scripts, settings, play history and the numbers you record (such as reps) are kept in Overtone on your iPhone, and in your iPhone's backups. Files you add from the Files app are copied into Overtone. The iPhone and your Apple Watch pass what's playing, and the workout being recorded, between them directly.

Health

If you allow it, Overtone records a workout in Apple Health while one plays, and reads your heart rate and the energy you burn from Health during it, to show them. It reads and writes nothing else. This stays in Health on your devices: Overtone never sends it anywhere, and never uses it for advertising. You can change what Overtone may use at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

What Overtone downloads

  • The workout library. When you open the library or New Workout's exercise list, Overtone downloads the list of workouts and exercises, and a workout when you add it, from this site, which GitHub hosts. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address with each request; see GitHub's privacy statement. No information about you is sent with it.
  • Natural voices. If you choose to download the natural voices, Overtone downloads them from Hugging Face, which likewise receives your IP address; see Hugging Face's privacy policy. Speech is then made on your iPhone: what your workouts say is never sent anywhere.

Children

Overtone collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here, with the date above.
