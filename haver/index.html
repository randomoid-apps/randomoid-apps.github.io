Haver Support Privacy

Notes, typed or said.

Talk for as long as you like. Haver writes it down on your iPhone, and Apple Intelligence gives it a title and tags, and tidies it when you ask.

What it does

  • Record or type. Send a message to start a note, or tap the microphone and talk. What you say is transcribed on the iPhone as you speak.
  • Titled and tagged for you, using the tags you already have, and tidied only when you ask, keeping what you said to go back to.
  • Folders with their own colours, search across titles, tags and text, and Markdown: headings, lists and checkboxes.
  • Protected folders, encrypted and hidden until you unlock them with Face ID or a PIN that isn't your iPhone's passcode.
  • Your notes are plain Markdown files, which other apps can open. Keep them in iCloud Drive to have them on your other devices, or export them all at any time.

Needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, set to English. Without Apple Intelligence, notes are still transcribed, but not titled, tagged or tidied.
