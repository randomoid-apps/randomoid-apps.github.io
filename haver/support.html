Common questions

My notes have no titles or tags.

Titles, tags and tidying need Apple Intelligence, turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri, with the iPhone set to English. Without it, notes are named by their first line.

How do I get my notes onto another device?

In Haver's Settings, choose a folder in iCloud Drive for the journal; it's moved there, and changes from other devices show up as they arrive. Recordings stay on the iPhone they were made on.

I've lost access to a protected folder.

Protected notes open with Face ID or their PIN, or on another device with the recovery key shown in Settings. Keep a copy of the recovery key somewhere safe: without the key, protected notes can't be read, by anyone.
