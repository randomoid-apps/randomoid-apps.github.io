Haver Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Haver doesn't collect any information about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, it sends nothing to us, and it makes no connection to any server of ours or anyone else's.

Recordings and transcription

With your permission, Haver uses the microphone while you record. Recordings stay on your iPhone, and are deleted after the time you choose in Settings (a month unless you change it), once they've been transcribed. Transcription is done on your iPhone by iOS's speech recognition; the first time, iOS may download its English speech model from Apple.

Apple Intelligence

Titles, tags and tidying are done by the Apple Intelligence model on your iPhone. What you write and say isn't sent anywhere for this.

Where your notes are kept

Notes are kept in Haver on your iPhone, and in your iPhone's backups. If you choose a folder for them in Settings, such as one in iCloud Drive, they're kept there instead, and iCloud Drive keeps them under Apple's privacy policy. Notes leave your iPhone otherwise only when you export or share them.

Protected folders and Face ID

Notes in protected folders are encrypted on your iPhone with a key kept in the Keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, iOS keeps that key on your other devices too, end-to-end encrypted by Apple. Face ID is handled by iOS: Haver is only told whether it succeeded, and never sees your face data.

Children

Haver collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here, with the date above.
